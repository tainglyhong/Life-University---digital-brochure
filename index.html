Grade B — Entrance Exam

Half tuition waiver for strong exam results.

  • Eligibility: Grade B in entrance exam
  • Covers: 50% tuition (annual)
  • Programs: All eligible bachelor majors

Korean Language Scholarship

Up to 80% tuition support for students in the Korean Language program.

  • Eligibility: Enrolled (or admitted) in Korean Language major
  • Covers: Up to 80% tuition (annual; limited quotas)
  • Requirements: Placement/interview & maintain academic standing
20%

Life School Students

20% tuition discount for eligible students from Life School.

  • Eligibility: Graduates/Grade 12 from Life School (proof required)
  • Covers: 20% tuition (first year, unless stated)
  • How to apply: Submit certification with your application
100% +

Theology Scholarship

Full tuition plus additional support for Theology students.

  • Covers: 100% tuition
  • Extras: Special financial/boarding support
  • Programs: Theology (B.A., M.Div., etc.)

Important

  • Awards are limited and may require annual renewal.
